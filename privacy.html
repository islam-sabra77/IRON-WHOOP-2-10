IRON privacy

IRON is a personal training app used by one person. When you connect WHOOP, IRON reads your recovery score, heart rate variability and resting heart rate so it can adjust your workouts.

Your WHOOP login is kept in a secure, private cookie in your own browser. WHOOP data is sent only between WHOOP, this website and your device. It is not sold, shared or stored on any other server.

You can disconnect at any time in IRON's Settings, which also revokes IRON's access in WHOOP.
