IRON privacy

Last updated 13 November 2026.

IRON is a personal training app. Your plan, workout logs, body measurements, supplements and habits are stored on your own phone. Nothing is sold, used for advertising or shared with anyone else.

Cloud backup

If you turn on cloud backup, your data is encrypted on your phone with a key made from your private sync code before it is sent. The server stores only the encrypted copy and cannot read it. Passkeys keep your sync code wrapped in a second layer of encryption that only your passkey can open.

WHOOP

When you connect WHOOP, IRON reads your recovery, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, sleep and workouts so it can adjust your training. Your WHOOP login is kept in a secure cookie in your own browser. You can disconnect at any time in Settings, which also revokes IRON's access in WHOOP.

InBody scans

When you upload an InBody photo, it is sent once to an AI model (Anthropic's Claude) to read the numbers. The photo is not stored by IRON, and Anthropic does not use it to train its models.

Arabic translations

In Arabic, on-screen text that has no built-in translation (such as exercise tips and supplement details) is translated once by Claude and cached. Only the on-screen wording is sent, never your logs or body data.

Notifications

For rest timer alerts and reminders, the server keeps your phone's notification address and the text and time of each alert until it is sent. Turning reminders off or deleting your data removes them.

Error reports and feedback

If something breaks, IRON sends a short error report (the error message, the screen you were on and the app version). Feedback you send includes only what you write plus the same details. Neither includes your workout or body data.

Abuse protection

To stop floods of requests, the server keeps short-lived counters for each device, keyed by a scrambled (hashed) form of its IP address. Raw IP addresses are not stored, and counters reset every day.

Your choices

See also the Terms of use. In IRON, open More, then Privacy and your data, to download everything IRON holds about you or to delete it. Deleting removes your data from this phone, deletes your cloud backup, disconnects WHOOP and stops reminders.
